rclone (personal Google Drive client)

This page describes a personal OAuth client registered for rclone, the open-source command-line program for managing files on cloud storage.

The client is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers, to back up and sync the owner's own files with the owner's own Google Drive. It is not a public service. It has no other users and is not offered or sold to anyone.

What it does

  • Uploads, downloads, lists, and syncs files between the owner's computers and the owner's Google Drive.
  • Runs only when the owner starts it, or on schedules the owner set up on the owner's own computers.

Privacy Policy
